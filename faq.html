Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some frequently asked questions about StackPug's web design services. If you have any other questions, please feel free to contact us directly.

What is StackPug?

StackPug is a web design service that specializes in creating professional, affordable websites for local businesses. We focus on simplicity, quality, and customer satisfaction.

What is a landing page?

A landing page is a standalone web page designed to convert visitors into leads or customers. It typically focuses on a single product or service and includes a clear call-to-action (CTA) to encourage visitors to take a specific action, such as filling out a form or making a purchase.

What is SEO?

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimisation. It helps your website appear in search results when people search for businesses like yours on Google.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page can take around 1 week. Larger websites with multiple pages usually take 2–4 weeks depending on complexity.

Do I need hosting?

Yes. Hosting stores your website online so people can access it. We can help you choose hosting and connect your domain.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. All StackPug websites are fully responsive and work smoothly on phones, tablets and desktops.

Can I update my website later?

Yes. We offer maintenance packages or can help update your site whenever you need changes.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance and support?

Yes, we offer ongoing maintenance and support packages to ensure your website remains up-to-date and secure. Our maintenance services include regular updates, backups, and technical support.

Can you help with content creation?

While we primarily focus on web design and development, we can offer guidance and recommendations for content creation.

Do you provide SEO services?

Yes, we can optimize your website for search engines to help improve your online visibility. Our SEO services include keyword research, on-page optimization, and technical SEO improvements.

What if I need to make changes to my website after it's launched?

We understand that businesses evolve and may need to make changes to their website over time. We offer ongoing maintenance and support packages that include updates and changes to your website. You can also contact us directly for any specific changes you need, and we will be happy to assist you.

Do you offer custom design services?

Yes, we offer custom design services to create a unique and personalized website that reflects your brand and meets your specific needs. We will work closely with you to understand your vision and create a design that aligns with your goals.

How do I get started?

Getting started is easy! Simply contact us to schedule a consultation. During the consultation, we will discuss your needs, goals, and budget to determine the best approach for your website. We will then provide you with a proposal and timeline for the project. Once you approve the proposal, we will begin the design and development process.
