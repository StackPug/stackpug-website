About StackPug

Hi, I'm Hayley 👋

Welcome to StackPug, a web design service built for local businesses, created by me, a self-taught developer based in Essex.

I always wanted to understand how the web actually worked, how to read and write code, not just drag and drop someone else's template. Teaching myself to build websites from scratch took time and determination, and I'm proud of every line of code.

I started StackPug because I kept seeing local businesses with outdated websites, or no website at all, losing customers to competitors who simply had a better online presence. I wanted to change that, one business at a time.

Being self-taught means I know how overwhelming the technical side of running a business can feel. So I keep things simple, no jargon, no confusion, just a website that works for you and your customers.

Whether you're starting from scratch or improving what you already have, I'll make the process straightforward, transparent, and stress-free, and I'll be with you every step of the way.

When I'm not coding, you'll usually find me with a cup of tea and Ginny, my pug. 🐾
