About StackPug

Welcome to StackPug, created by Hayley, a self-taught web developer based in Essex.

I always wanted to understand how the web worked — how to actually read and write code, not just use someone else's tools. Teaching myself to build websites from scratch took time and determination, but being able to call myself a developer genuinely means a lot to me.

I started StackPug because I kept seeing local businesses with outdated websites — or no website at all — losing customers to competitors who simply had a better online presence. I wanted to change that, one business at a time.

Being self-taught means I know how overwhelming the technical side of running a business can feel. So I keep things simple — no jargon, no confusion, just a website that works for you and your customers.

Whether you're starting from scratch or improving what you already have, I'll make the process straightforward, transparent, and stress-free.
