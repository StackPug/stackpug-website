Services

StackPug offers simple, clean responsive websites built for local people. We specialize in creating websites for local businesses, ensuring they have a strong online presence to attract and engage customers in their community. Our current websites are built with HTML and CSS. We can also use Wordpress and Shopify. Here at StackPug, we are always learning and expanding our skills to offer even better services in the future.

What we offer

Front-End Websites

Simple, responsive websites built with HTML, CSS, Wordpress, Shopify. Our prices range from £300-£800 depending on the size and complexity of the site.

Styling & Layout

Improving current design, spacing, and usability of existing pages. Prices start at £100 per page.

Accessibility

Building sites that are readable, usable, and inclusive. Prices start at £100 per page.

Maintenance

Ongoing updates, fixes, and support. Monthly maintenance packages start at £20-£50 per month. This includes small updates to content, performance improvements. Equivalent to maximum of 2 hours of work per month.

Hosting

Your website needs a place to live on the internet. We offer hosting packages starting at £5/month.
